01 / About

From early-stage ideas
to market traction.

My sweet spot

Bringing structure to early-stage ideas, translating technical or unfamiliar topics for an audience, and connecting content, partnerships and digital journeys to business goals.

I work at the intersection of growth, marketing and business development — helping brands clarify their positioning, connect with the right audiences and turn opportunities into practical plans. My experience spans consumer platforms, F&B, hospitality, premium B2B brands and emerging technology.

My work spans 0-to-1 product and go-to-market initiatives, community-led growth, content strategy, brand partnerships and digital marketing. Across my projects, I have built a 29.6K+ community, onboarded 500+ restaurants to a digital platform and developed campaigns and strategies for different business audiences.

What I enjoy most is building something from the ground up: finding the story, understanding the audience, bringing the idea to market and seeing what resonates. The industries may change, but that part never gets old.
