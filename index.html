01 / About

Strategic thinking.
Hands-on execution.

My sweet spot

Bringing structure to early-stage ideas, translating technical or unfamiliar topics for an audience, and connecting content, partnerships and digital journeys to business goals.

YOUR PHOTO HEREMaryam in action · speaking, facilitating or building

I work across the space between product, marketing and community — drawing on an international business foundation from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) to help teams turn opportunities into clear stories, focused campaigns and measurable growth.

My experience spans consumer platforms, hospitality, product development and Web3 education. I've built communities, developed brand partnerships, shaped go-to-market narratives and experimented with AI-assisted content workflows.

Earlier in my career, I worked on regional social media and brand communications at The Executive Centre, a premium flexible-workspace provider serving multinational companies, enterprise teams and high-net-worth professionals. That experience strengthened my understanding of premium audiences, corporate messaging and regional brand consistency.

I combine audience understanding and clear positioning with practical execution: defining the message, choosing the right channels, building the campaign and deciding how success should be measured.
