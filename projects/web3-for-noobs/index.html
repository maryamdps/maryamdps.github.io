01 / The challenge

Make the first step
feel approachable.

Web3 can feel abstract and jargon-heavy to newcomers. The project focused on translating unfamiliar concepts into accessible content and giving beginners practical ways to continue learning.

Audience needs

  • Plain-language explanations.
  • Concrete examples and walkthroughs.
  • Recognisable visuals across topics.
  • Clear next steps into resources and community.
Objective

Build confidence through learning.

Use approachable explainers, practical tutorials, partner-led sessions and community touchpoints to help beginners move from curiosity to understanding.

05 / My role

End-to-end project
leadership.

I led Web3 For Noobs across strategy and hands-on execution — shaping the project direction, building its educational content system, developing its visual identity and digital presence, and connecting content with partnerships, community and events.

Strategy, brand & content

  • Defined the beginner-focused positioning, content direction and educational pillars.
  • Researched Web3 topics and translated technical concepts into posts, carousels, reels, explainers, memes, quizzes and tutorials.
  • Developed the brand’s visual language and AI-assisted mascot system; created and adapted visuals for different topics.
  • Built the project’s digital presence and learning resources, including its website and dictionary.
  • Used AI tools including ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, Gemini and HeyGen across research, ideation, scripting and content production.

Partnerships, community & operations

  • Identified and coordinated collaborations and co-marketing with Web3 businesses and partners.
  • Managed Telegram community touchpoints and bot integrations.
  • Developed and hosted learning sessions, including Stablecoins 101 and an educational X Spaces series.
  • Managed event planning and promotion, registration, ticketing, attendee communications and onboarding through Luma and Eventbrite.
  • Coordinated partner speakers, sponsored prizes, NFT credentials and attendee merchandise; secured event listing on the HKWeb3 Events Calendar.
