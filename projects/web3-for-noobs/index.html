01 / The challenge

Make the first step
feel approachable.

Web3 can feel abstract and jargon-heavy to people who are new to the space. The content needed to help beginners discover useful concepts without assuming prior knowledge, while giving them clear next steps to keep learning.

The approach shown here combines accessible educational topics, a recognisable visual thread, practical demonstrations and community touchpoints. This page focuses on the work and content formats rather than claiming unverified business outcomes.

What the audience needed

  • Plain-language explanations of unfamiliar concepts.
  • Concrete examples and practical walkthroughs.
  • Consistent visual cues to make educational posts recognisable.
  • Clear routes to continue learning or connect with the project.
Working principle

Reduce friction before asking for commitment.

Start with a question or concept a beginner can recognise, explain it simply, and offer a next step such as a tutorial, related post, event or community channel.

02 / The approach

Content that teaches,
not just announces.

The work brings together beginner-friendly explainers, a hands-on MetaMask tutorial, a recurring mascot adapted to different subjects, and partner-led learning sessions that connect concepts with real-world use cases.

Educational content pillars

  • Web3 concepts explained in accessible language.
  • Practical how-to content for getting started.
  • Timely topics and emerging concepts, translated for beginners.
  • Case-study and news-style posts to connect concepts with real examples.

Community and discovery

  • Social content that can be discovered and shared organically.
  • Community touchpoints including Telegram and Zealy.
  • Community events and learning sessions that invite participation.
  • Partner collaborations that bring subject-matter expertise, incentives and new perspectives to the audience.

04 / Partnership spotlight

Stablecoins 101,
with KIRAYPAY.

The second learning session, Stablecoins 101, explored what stablecoins are, where the technology may be heading, and how they are used in real-world settings. The session was designed to make the topic more tangible for people learning about Web3.

KIRAYPAY partnered on the session as a guest speaker, bringing a partner perspective to the discussion. The collaboration extended beyond the talk: KIRAYPAY sponsored prizes for quiz winners, while an NFT was created for the attendance certificate and a separate winners badge.

Partner collaboration

Learning, participation and recognition.

The session combined educational content with audience interaction and partner involvement — giving attendees a reason to participate and a digital keepsake to mark their involvement.

What the partnership included

  • Guest speaker: KIRAYPAY contributed to the Stablecoins 101 session.
  • Educational focus: stablecoin fundamentals, future direction and real-world use cases.
  • Quiz prizes: sponsored by KIRAYPAY for quiz winners.
  • NFT assets: an attendance-certificate NFT and a winners-badge NFT.

06 / Visual identity

A familiar face
for new concepts.

A recurring robot mascot was generated with Google Gemini and adapted to suit different educational topics. The intent is to give the content a recognisable visual thread without making every post look identical. A larger showcase of the original character alongside topic-specific variations will make that system easier to see at a glance.

Contribution described: AI-assisted character generation, visual adaptation and application across educational content.

07 / What this demonstrates

A repeatable approach
to education-led growth.

This project demonstrates how educational content, practical tutorials, recurring visual identity and community touchpoints can work together as a beginner-facing discovery experience.

The current public evidence supports the content formats and the reported organic-view figure above. Conversion, retention, community growth attributable to individual posts, and other business results are not claimed here because verified figures were not supplied.

Capabilities shown

  • Translating technical ideas into accessible content.
  • Planning content across multiple formats.
  • Using AI-assisted workflows for visual development.
  • Connecting content with learning resources and community touchpoints.

Useful next measurements

  • Reach and saves/shares by content pillar.
  • Video retention and click-throughs to learning resources.
  • Clicks from social content to community channels.
  • New members or sign-ups attributed to specific campaigns.

These are recommended future measurements, not reported results.
