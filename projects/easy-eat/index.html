Case study 03 / Digital product & venture building

Making inclusive dining easier to find.

Easy Eat was a restaurant discovery platform designed to help people with different dietary needs find places to eat in Hong Kong — with filters that made the search more relevant and practical.

Easy Eat · Hong Kong
Project overview
Purpose

Make it easier to discover restaurants that accommodate different dietary requirements.

Market

Hong Kong diners with dietary preferences, restrictions and specific food needs.

Role

Managing Director · Product direction, restaurant onboarding and launch growth.

Stage

Beta launch and early user acquisition. The venture has since closed.

01 / The experience

Restaurant discovery built around dietary needs.

Rather than making users search through general restaurant listings, Easy Eat brought dietary information into the discovery journey. Users could narrow options based on what mattered to them, compare suitable places and use a map to make location-based decisions.

01 / FILTER

Dietary & preference filters

Browse and filter restaurants by dietary needs, price range, cuisine and other preferences.

02 / DISCOVER

Relevant restaurant listings

Explore restaurant options through a browsing experience designed to make dietary suitability easier to assess.

03 / LOCATE

Map-based discovery

View restaurant locations on a map and filter by preferred areas to find convenient options.

04 / My role

Building the platform and its supply side.

As Managing Director, I worked across the product experience and the restaurant network, helping shape the platform around real dietary discovery needs and supporting its beta launch.

Product & user experience

  • Helped shape the restaurant discovery experience around dietary needs and user preferences.
  • Defined key filters and browsing considerations, including dietary requirements, cuisine, price and location.
  • Supported the map-based discovery journey to help users find suitable restaurants by area.

Growth & restaurant onboarding

  • Led restaurant onboarding and development of the platform's restaurant supply.
  • Supported beta-launch visibility and audience development through social channels.
  • Monitored early website engagement and audience signals to understand initial product traction.

The platform is presented as a completed venture case study, not as an active product or current service.
